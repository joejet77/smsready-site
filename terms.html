What SMS Ready is

SMS Ready provides software, document templates generated from your answers, reminders, and record-keeping to help a Part 135 certificate holder develop, implement, and document a Safety Management System under 14 CFR Part 5. SMS Ready is an independent product, not affiliated with the FAA.

What it is not

SMS Ready does not certify compliance, does not represent you to the FAA, and does not provide legal advice. Compliance with Part 5, the content and signature of any declaration of compliance, and the outcome of FAA surveillance remain the responsibility of the certificate holder and its accountable executive.

Privacy

The free gap check stores nothing: your answers stay in your browser. Subscribers' records are stored encrypted, belong to the subscriber, are exportable at any time, and are never shared with the FAA or any third party except as the subscriber directs or the law requires. Full policy to follow.
